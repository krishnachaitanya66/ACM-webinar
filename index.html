What can you build, contribute, and achieve while still in college?

Join us for an exclusive interactive session with Ashutosh Singh — 2× GSoC contributor, LFX mentee, and code owner at Sugar Labs with 100+ merged PRs across 12+ global open-source organizations (including KDE, Joplin, and FOSSASIA). As the creator of VengeanceUI (500K+ monthly views, 1,250+ GitHub stars) and core contributor to AI-agent platform architectures, he will share firsthand insights on cracking top developer programs (GSoC, LFX, C4GT), building high-impact open-source projects, and transforming technical skills into real-world career opportunities.
